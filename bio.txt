I come from a town that sits far from the city lights, yet I'm headed to a city that never stops building.

I carry a name that's also a dragon's. Online, I'm Ryu, and I write in code instead of fire.

My hands know two languages: one of pencils and color, the other of logic and syntax. I'm never sure which one I'm speaking until the work is done.

I'm a student of learning itself. Before I master a subject, I study how to master it.

Two years of waiting stand behind me, and a classroom of machines that learn stands ahead. When August comes, I'll sit among them and learn how they think.

My compass points east, past the horizon, to the land of the rising sun. I'm not there yet, but I'm building the road.

Who am I?
